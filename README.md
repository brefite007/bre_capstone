# Eating Smarter: The Shift Toward Cost-Conscious Consumption
**Author:** Bre Renner  
**Repository:** `bre_capstone`  
**Tools Used:** Python, Excel, Tableau, Canva, Google Forms 

---

###  Project Overview
This project explores how the COVID-19 pandemic reshaped food habits in the United States, especially how people are shifting toward more cost-conscious decisions around eating. With a focus on national trends and local insights from West Virginia, this project aims to understand the growing trend of home cooking and its financial impact.

---

###  Goals and Objectives

- **Analyze Inflation Trends**  
  Use Consumer Price Index (CPI) data to compare price changes in grocery vs. restaurant spending.

- **Understand Consumer Behavior**  
  Examine how individuals and families are adjusting their food budgets across different store types.

- **Demographic & Regional Insights**  
  Evaluate how factors like age, income, and region influence food choices and spending.

- **Local Focus – West Virginia**  
  Compare local eating and spending patterns to national trends from a survey.

- **Cost & Time Analysis**  
  Break down the average cost and time required to prepare meals at home vs. dining out.

- **Strategic Insights**  
  Provide actionable insights for both consumers looking to save money and for the food industry to adapt to new trends.
